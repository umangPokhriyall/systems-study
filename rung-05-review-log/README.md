# Rung 5 - the review muscle

**Not started.** Runs continuously once rung 1 is complete, rather than in sequence.

## What will land here

One entry per merged pull request read in Cloud Hypervisor, Firecracker or a rust-vmm crate,
including the review conversation, not just the diff. Each entry records:

- what the patch did, in one sentence
- what the maintainer objected to, quoted
- what the author had to change
- what that implies about the project's standards

Fifteen minutes per working day. The subject matter is other people, which is why it is the one rung
that never finishes.

## Why it exists

Everything else in this ladder is verifiable alone. This is the only part that teaches what a
maintainer in these specific projects actually pushes back on - which is not guessable from the
contribution guide, and is the difference between a first patch that lands and one that stalls.
